# Excel Projects
A collection of Excel projects showcasing data analysis and visualization to enhance productivity and decision-making

## Project 1: Developer's Salary Dashboard in Norway, 2024

![Untitled design](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f6b42e8b-6044-4f7e-9fc4-302ec95d73b5)

### Description
This salary dashboard was created to facilitate the exploration of various job opportunities across different regions of Norway, taking into account factors such as job title and years of experience.

### Challenge
Due to a change in the county division effective from 1. January 2024, for correct data visualization in Excel salary information was changed in the following way:

- Data from Viken county was extrapolated to Akershus, Buskerud and Østfold counties. 

- information from "Vestfold and Telemark" county was extrapolated to the separate counties of Vestfold and Telemark.

- The separate counties of Troms and Finnmark have salary information as "Troms and Finnmark" county.

While these updates may lead to slight inaccuracies in the salary and job data, they are sufficient to provide a broad overview of the information.
